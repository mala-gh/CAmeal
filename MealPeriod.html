What the calculation uses

  • A first 30-minute meal must generally be provided when work exceeds 5 hours.
  • The first meal must begin no later than the end of the 5th hour worked.
  • The first meal may be waived by mutual consent only when total work is no more than 6 hours.
  • A second 30-minute meal must generally be provided when work exceeds 10 hours.
  • The second meal may be waived by mutual consent only when total work is no more than 12 hours and the first meal was not waived.

General rule for nonexempt employees. Industry, occupation, wage-order, or collective-bargaining exceptions may apply. This calculator does not cover paid rest breaks.
